WHAT THE POSITIONS DO

QB
Quarterback = main DPSThrows footballs from range. Usually targets the enemy QB first. Losing him hurts because you lose your main damage dealer.
OL
Offensive line = rusherCharges through the fight toward the enemy quarterback and tries to knock him out.
DL
Defensive line = protectorStays near your QB, attacks rushers that enter the pocket, and reduces damage while close to him.

Win condition: beat the snot out of everyone on the other team. Practical strategy: knock out their QB while keeping yours standing.
